> [!IMPORTANT]
> **BASE PAPER (S.No 1):** "SustainNet: A Cloud-Based Surplus Food Redistribution Network with Geospatial Matching" was selected as the base paper because it shares the highest architectural overlap with EGPP — PostGIS spatial queries, React frontend, JWT auth, cloud-native microservices, and real-time geospatial food surplus matching. Its limitations (no push notifications, no urgency scoring, no ephemeral data design, no privacy compliance) are precisely the gaps that EGPP addresses, making it the ideal foundation to build upon.
